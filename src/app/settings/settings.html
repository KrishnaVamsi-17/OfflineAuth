Offline TOTP Authenticator

Manage your offline Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) accounts for 2FA without internet.

🔐 How it works:

  • Scan QR codes from Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, or Google Authenticator
  • Secrets are encrypted with your PIN and stored locally
  • Generate 6-digit codes offline, anytime
  • Each code is valid for 30 seconds
  • Backup codes are provided for account recovery

⚠️ Important:

  • Set a PIN before adding TOTP accounts
  • Back up your recovery codes in a safe place
  • This is for offline use only - test with online services first
